Privacy policy · ZA Logistics Bot

ZA Logistics Bot is a private Telegram bot used by the ZA Home Café 2.0 committee to track event logistics.

What it stores

  • Item names, quantities and prices that committee members send to the bot.
  • The names people pick or type for who paid or who lent an item.
  • Receipt photos and payback screenshots that members send to the bot.
  • Which Telegram group chat belongs to which team.

Where it is stored

Everything is written to the committee's own Google Sheet and a Google Drive folder called "ZA Receipts". The bot only uses Google access to edit that sheet and the files it creates itself. It does not read any other files in the Google account.

Sharing

The data is only seen by committee members who have access to the sheet and folder. It is not sold or shared with anyone else.

Deleting data

Ask a committee admin to remove your rows or receipts from the sheet and folder. After the event, the committee can delete the sheet and folder.
